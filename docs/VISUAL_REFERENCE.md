# PatiLife Approved Visual Reference

Binary source of truth: `docs/assets/patilife-design-reference.jpg`.

The approved board contains ten mobile concepts:
1. full-screen warm opening hero
2. two-column species onboarding
3. Home/Today with large pet hero, timeline and five quick actions
4. quick weight bottom sheet
5. photo-led pet profile
6. health overview with weight trend
7. vaccine tracking
8. diary/memories timeline
9. statistics/trends
10. dark-mode Home

## Exact character
- warm off-white background around `#F7F7F4`
- near-black primary text around `#171717`
- sage primary around `#6F9E86`
- muted peach secondary around `#E8A87C`
- dark background around `#111211`
- raised dark surface around `#1B1D1B`
- 20–32 px rounded content surfaces
- little shadow
- system-like confident typography
- large pet imagery
- four primary tabs
- no giant center FAB
- health/lifestyle premium feel rather than cartoon pet utility

The footer word “PawLife” visible in the old concept board is obsolete. The product name is **PatiLife**.
